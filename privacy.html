RentBestie

Privacy Policy

RentBestie has no account, no analytics and no server. The AI model is inside the app and runs on your device, so your conversations are never sent anywhere.

Last updated: 19 September 2026.

The short version. We collect nothing. We receive nothing. The app contains no networking code at all — it cannot send your messages anywhere, even if we wanted it to. Everything you do in RentBestie stays in the app's own storage on your device, and is deleted when you delete the app.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no email capture, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting and no device identifier of any kind. We operate no server that receives data from the app.

What the app stores on your device

WhatWhereWhy
Your conversations The app's private storage So a chat is still there when you come back to it
Who you marked Interested or Not interested App preferences So the deck does not show you the same character twice
Your appearance choice (dark or light) App preferences So the app opens the way you left it

None of this is transmitted. Conversations are additionally marked as excluded from iCloud and computer backups, so a transcript does not travel off the device that way either.

How the AI works

The language model is Llama 3.2 1B Instruct from Meta, quantised and shipped inside the app. Replies are generated on your device's own processor. There is no cloud fallback and no API key to add — the app works in airplane mode, and works identically whether or not you have a connection.

Permissions we do not ask for

RentBestie requests no permissions at all. It does not access your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, location, calendar, health data or Bluetooth. The character photographs are bundled inside the app; the app never reads from or writes to your photo library, so nothing from RentBestie appears in your gallery.

Children

RentBestie is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect information at all.

The characters

Every companion in RentBestie is fictional. The photographs are AI-generated and depict no real person. Names, ages, home towns, occupations and life stories are invented, and the places they come from do not exist. Replies are produced by a language model and are not statements of fact.

Deleting your data

Settings inside the app let you delete every conversation, or erase everything including your Interested and Not interested lists. Deleting the app removes all of it from your device. Because we never receive any of it, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and nothing to request from us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published here with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
